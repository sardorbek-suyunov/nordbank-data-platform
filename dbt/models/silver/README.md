# dbt/models/silver

Silver models, named `sl_<entity>` (specification 008).

Conformed, business-keyed entities: deduplicated to the earliest batch of each version, SCD2
where a question reads the entity's history (`_valid_from`, `_valid_to`, `_is_current`, the first
version at the epoch), latest state otherwise, soft deletes applied, amounts converted to EUR
with their rate provenance, and timestamps in UTC. Identifiers arrive already tokenised from
ingest; quasi-identifiers stay in the clear here, beside their generalisations. Joins across
sources are allowed; aggregation to a reporting grain is not. Tables rebuilt in full, every
contract enforced.

Three kinds of file, and who writes each:

- `sl_<ref table>.sql` and `sl_agent_locations.sql`, with their `.yml`: generated by
  `make silver-generate` from the contracts, one call to `scd2` each. Never edited by hand.
- `sl_<entity>.yml` for the other models that mirror one entity: generated likewise, while their
  SQL is written by hand, because it does more than historise.
- `sl_fx_rates`, `sl_account_balance_observations` and the two band models: written by hand,
  SQL and properties, because they mirror no single entity.

The rules they apply are stated once: versioning in ADR 0020 and `macros/historisation.sql`,
conversion in `architecture.md`, ADR 0019 and `macros/fx.sql`.
